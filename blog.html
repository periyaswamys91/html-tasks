Welcome to My Personal Journey

Introduction

My name is Periyaswamy. I was born in Tuticorin, Tamil Nadu. I completed my elementary education at a local school in chennai.
From my childhood, I was interested in learning new things and participating in school activities. My teachers and parents encouraged me to develop good habits and work hard toward my goals.

Education Details

I completed my high school education at Corporation Higher Secondary School, where I excelled in Commerce and Computer Application.

After that, I pursued a Bachelor's degree in Computer Application from New Prince Shri Bhavani Arts and Science College. During my college years, I actively participated in coding competitions and workshops, which helped me enhance my technical skills.

Achievements

Throughout my academic journey, I have received several awards and recognitions for my performance in various competitions. I was awarded the Best Student of the Year in my final year of college. Additionally, I have completed several online courses and certifications to further improve my knowledge in the field of technology.

Special Skills

I possess strong programming skills in languages such as Python, Java, and C++. I am also proficient in web development technologies like HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. My problem-solving abilities and attention to detail have been instrumental in my academic and professional success.

Conclusion
